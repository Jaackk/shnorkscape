## Reviewed and retained

- **Fishing:** original fish tables already select unlocked fish with a level-sensitive roll, require the matched tool/bait, consume bait only on an atomic successful catch, stop with a full backpack or moved/despawned spot, and cancel scene movement. No rate or recipe changes were justified in this pass. Toolbelt, alternate feather/bait choices, aquarium perks, deep-sea methods, barbarian fishing and moving-spot schedules still need broader implementation.
- **Divination:** harvesting, enriched memory treatment, three conversion modes, five/ten energy costs and fallback from enhanced XP when energy runs out are already present. The existing action owns the paused wisp and restores it on stop. No behavior changes this pass. Chronicle fragments, boons, timespecific enrichment, hall-of-memories and broad alternate training remain incomplete.
- **Hunter and implings:** reviewed net/jar requirements, original catch and loot tables, inventory transactions, cancellation, butterfly release, NPC respawn and impling jar loot. Current net-and-jar loops remain intact. Barehanded capture, trap-based creatures, Puro-Puro differences, Daffyd rewards, butterflies used on other players and newer creature families are outside this bounded pass. This does not claim all retail Hunter training methods are implemented.
